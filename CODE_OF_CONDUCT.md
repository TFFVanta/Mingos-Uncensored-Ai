# Code of Conduct

Mingo's Uncensored AI is created by Mingo and built with its community. To keep the project welcoming,
everyone taking part (in issues, discussions and pull requests) agrees to:

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people. No insults, harassment or personal attacks.
- **Be welcoming.** Help newcomers. Nobody should be made to feel unwelcome because of who they are.
- **Stay on topic.** Keep project spaces focused on the project.
- **Don't share harmful content** in project spaces, including content that breaks the
  [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE_USE.md). The app being uncensored doesn't make the community
  spaces uncensored.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't post other people's personal information, or your own chat logs
  without checking them for private details.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block people who
repeatedly do. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through the repository's GitHub page.
