What is uncensored AI?

Most AI assistants are trained to refuse certain requests. That helps sometimes, but it also misfires: they turn down harmless questions, lecture, or won't write fiction with dark themes.

An uncensored model has those trained-in refusals removed. This app's default models are "abliterated": researchers found that refusing is mostly controlled by one direction inside the model, and abliteration removes it. The model keeps everything it knew, but it answers instead of refusing.

It doesn't make the model smarter or more accurate. It can still be wrong. It doesn't change the law either: you're responsible for what you create, and this app never generates sexual images involving minors.

Why use it?
  • Privacy: confidential work, personal matters and health questions stay on your PC.
  • Straight answers on medicine, law, security, sexuality, history and politics, without hedging.
  • Creative freedom: fiction and roleplay can go wherever the story needs.
  • Control: choose your models, see exactly what the AI was told, and work offline with no limits or policy changes.

How agents work

The sandbox includes numpy, pandas and matplotlib, and needs no approval.
SearXNG sends your search words to engines like DuckDuckGo, Google and Bing, but without cookies or anything that identifies you.
Stops an agent from looping forever.

Modes: 🐝 Team: a planner splits the job, specialists do their parts, a reviewer checks and writes the answer. 👑 Lead: a lead agent hires helpers one at a time, even inventing new ones, and decides as it goes. ⚖️ Debate: agents answer separately, critique each other, and a judge writes the best answer. Agents take turns on your graphics card, so a swarm takes longer than a single reply.

MCP tool servers

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard for plugging tools into AI agents. Each server you turn on adds tools that agents can use (the 🧭 Generalist gets all of them). Servers are open-source programs downloaded from npm when first turned on, and they run on your PC. Tools from servers you don't mark as trusted always ask before running.

Advanced: edit the server list (JSON)
Each server: {"command": "npx" or a program, "args": [...], "env": {...}, "enabled": true, "trusted": false}.
